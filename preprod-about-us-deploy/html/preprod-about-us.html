About Sinergify

Enabling cross-team collaboration to meet customer expectations is no easy task. Sinergify breaks down silos by connecting customer-facing and backend teams on a single platform. By seamlessly integrating CRM systems like Salesforce and Zendesk with Jira, Sinergify provides complete visibility into the buyer’s journey and helps teams boost productivity through seamless Salesforce–Jira integration. This enables faster issue resolution, saves time, and delivers personalized customer support that helps businesses stand apart.

Grazitti is a global leader in digital innovation, serving over 1,100 clients worldwide, including Fortune 500 companies across technology, banking, healthcare, and travel. Sinergify is powered by this expertise and legacy, ensuring efficiency and business success.
